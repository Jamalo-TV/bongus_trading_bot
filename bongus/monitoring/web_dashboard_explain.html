If you only read one thing

What Bongus actually does

This is the short version. It answers what this thing is trying to do and why it is not just a random gambling button.
Money idea

Collect the payment stream

Some perpetual futures pay or charge funding. Bongus hunts for the symbols where that payment looks attractive, then tries to hold the offsetting position long enough for the fee to be worth the friction.

--%
Risk idea

Try to stay market-neutral

It is not mainly betting that price will explode up or down. The design goal is to care more about the funding carry than the market's mood swings.

WAITING
Decision idea

Only rotate when it is worth it

If a shinier symbol appears, Bongus does not jump blindly. It checks whether the new opportunity should pay back the switch costs fast enough.

8h gate
Operator idea

Say "no" when the proof is weak

The bot grades itself. If performance, uptime, or health do not meet the current bar, it shows NO_GO or ADJUST instead of pretending everything is perfect.

Why people build this

It is a calm-harvester idea

Instead of asking "which coin will moon?", this kind of bot asks "where is the exchange paying a weird little carry, and can I collect it without taking silly risk?"

Quick map

The whole bot in human language

These are the main jobs inside Bongus. Think of them like team members, each with a clear role.
Plain English
Find

Look for juicy funding

Bongus scans symbols, checks funding, and throws away weak or unsafe ideas before they become trades.

Choose

Pick the best seats

It only has a handful of portfolio slots, so it spends them on the strongest candidates instead of everywhere at once.

Execute

Send orders carefully

The Python side decides what should happen. The Rust side handles the fast order plumbing and market event stream.

Record

Write down what happened

Trades, fills, metrics, and state snapshots go into storage so the dashboard and supervisor can tell the truth.

Guard

Watch for danger

Risk checks, kill switches, stale data alarms, and health samples try to stop the bot from acting brave when it should act boring.

Explain

Show you what is going on

The dashboard, logs, reports, and Telegram commands are the "talk back to the human" parts of the stack.
