Bongus Profitability Masterplan

  The Fundamental Math Problem

  Before the plan, understand the ceiling you're fighting against:

  Current theoretical max:
    4 slots × $5,000 notional × 15% avg funding = $3,000/year gross
    Minus round-trip costs: ~$120/month at current rotation frequency
    Net: ~$130-180/month on $10,000 capital = ~18% annualized

  To make this meaningfully more profitable you need to attack 4 levers:
    1. More notional deployed (leverage + compounding)
    2. More opportunities captured (more symbols, inverse funding)
    3. Lower cost per trade (fees, slippage)
    4. Better timing (enter higher, exit cleaner)

  ---
  Phase 1 — Free Money (1-2 Weeks, Zero Infrastructure Change)

  1A. Activate BNB Fee Discount + Verify It's Working

  The bot currently hardcodes MAKER_FEE_SPOT = 0.00075 (0.075%) but claims it's "w/ BNB discount." BNB discount is 25%
  off — the actual number should be 0.05625%. Verify in your Binance account that BNB discount is toggled ON and update
  config.py. This is a free 25% reduction in your largest cost line.

  Dollar impact at current volume: Every $5k entry/exit costs $3.75 in spot fees at 0.075%, vs $2.81 at 0.05625%. Small
  per trade, meaningful over a month.

  1B. Enable Inverse Funding Mode

  This is the most overlooked free alpha. When funding is negative (perp trades below spot), longs pay shorts. You flip
  the trade: short spot (margin borrow) + long perp. You collect the negative funding.

  Negative funding occurs during:
  - Bear market sentiment (common and sustained)
  - Post-liquidation cascades
  - Meme coin death spirals

  Currently, the bot sits idle during negative funding periods. Enabling this roughly doubles your opportunity set at
  zero additional capital cost, because the same 4 slots can be used for either direction.

  What needs to be built:
  - New AlphaInstruction intents: ENTER_SHORT / EXIT_SHORT
  - Rust execution: SELL_MARGIN (borrow spot, sell) + BUY_PERP
  - Strategy: enter when ann_funding < -ENTRY_ANN_FUNDING_THRESHOLD
  - Adjust borrow cost: spot margin borrowing at ~10% annual reduces net yield

  Dollar impact: Historically, negative funding periods on BTC/ETH average 15-30 days/year at rates exceeding 15%
  annualized. That's additional periods where the bot currently earns $0 but could earn.

  1C. Expand Symbol Universe From 8 to Dynamic Top-30

  The FundingRanker already fetches ALL Binance perp symbols in one REST call — you just filter to 8. Remove that
  filter. Let it rank all 300+ symbols. The liquidity filter (depth >= 5× notional) naturally eliminates the untradeable
   ones.

  High funding symbols you're currently missing:
  - WIFUSDT, BONKUSDT, JUPUSDT — meme coins routinely hit 100-500% annualized funding during hype cycles
  - New listings — first 72 hours after a new perp lists, funding is consistently extreme

  The WS depth subscription problem (can't subscribe to 300 streams): subscribe depth WS only to the top 15 by current
  funding rate after the liquidity pre-filter. Rotate subscriptions as rankings change every 60 seconds.

  Dollar impact: On any given day, the top-3 non-BTC symbols by funding rate typically exceed BTC's rate by 2-5x.
  Capturing one WIFUSDT slot at 100% funding instead of a BTCUSDT slot at 15% is 6.7× better alpha on that slot.

  Phase 2 — Capital Efficiency (2-4 Weeks)

  2A. Dynamic Leverage Scaling

  Fixed 2x leverage regardless of funding rate is deeply suboptimal. A delta-neutral position's primary risk is basis
  deviation, not spot price movement. The current basis deviation stop (0.3%) limits loss to ~$15 on a $5k notional
  position regardless of leverage.

  Plan — scale leverage with funding magnitude:

  ┌───────────────────────────┬──────────┬──────────────────────────┐
  │ Funding Rate (Annualized) │ Leverage │ Notional per $2,500 slot │
  ├───────────────────────────┼──────────┼──────────────────────────┤
  │ 15-25%                    │ 2x       │ $5,000                   │
  ├───────────────────────────┼──────────┼──────────────────────────┤
  │ 25-50%                    │ 3x       │ $7,500                   │
  ├───────────────────────────┼──────────┼──────────────────────────┤
  │ 50-100%                   │ 4x       │ $10,000                  │
  ├───────────────────────────┼──────────┼──────────────────────────┤
  │ >100%                     │ 5x       │ $12,500                  │
  └───────────────────────────┴──────────┴──────────────────────────┘

  At 100% funding with 4x leverage on a $2,500 slot:
  $10,000 notional × (100%/1095 per snapshot) = $9.13/snapshot × 3/day = $27.40/day
  vs current 2x: $5,000 × 15% / 365 = $2.05/day

  That's 13× better on high-rate days. Risk is still bounded by basis deviation stop, not liquidation from spot price
  movement.

  2B. Auto-Compounding

  Currently CAPITAL_PER_SLOT_USD = 2_500 is hardcoded. The bot earns money but never reinvests it.

  Plan: every 24 hours, PortfolioAllocator reads actual account equity from the Rust engine's
  UnifiedPortfolioMarginCalculator and recalculates CAPITAL_PER_SLOT_USD = account_equity / MAX_CONCURRENT_POSITIONS
  dynamically. As equity grows, position sizes grow.

  Compounding effect at 20% monthly return on capital:
  Month 1: $10,000
  Month 3: $17,280
  Month 6: $29,860
  Month 12: $89,161
  Without compounding you'd earn a flat ~$1,800/month forever. With compounding, month 12 earns ~$14,000 that month
  alone.

  2C. POST_ONLY Maker Orders in the Chase System

  The Rust chase system currently places limit orders but does not set POST_ONLY flag. Without POST_ONLY, if your limit
  price crosses the spread, Binance fills it as a taker and charges the taker fee. This silently degrades every entry
  where the market moves against you between order submission and fill.

  Adding timeInForce=GTX (Good Till Crossing) to both spot and perp limit orders guarantees maker fills or cancels —
  never accidental taker. Combined with the existing fallback to market only when the legging timeout triggers, this
  pushes maker fill rate from ~70% toward 90%+.

  Dollar impact: Moving from 70% to 90% maker fill rate on $5k notional:
  Current blended cost ≈ (0.70 × 0.012% + 0.30 × 0.075%) = 0.0309% per leg
  New blended cost ≈ (0.90 × 0.012% + 0.10 × 0.075%) = 0.0183% per leg
  Savings per round-trip: 4 legs × 0.0126% × $5,000 = $2.52/trade
  At 30 trades/month: $75.60/month saved

  2D. Snapshot Snipe Window Tightening

  Currently enters 60-120 minutes before snapshot. The now-live real-time funding feed (from the markPriceUpdate WS we
  just added) means you have accurate rate data up to 1 second before the snapshot.

  Plan: tighten the snipe window to 15-30 minutes before snapshot. Benefits:
  - Less time for basis to deviate against you between entry and settlement
  - Lower borrow cost (shorter hold)
  - Higher throughput: you can potentially snipe all 3 daily snapshots across multiple symbols
  - Exit 5-10 minutes after snapshot once funding decay is confirmed

  At 3 snapshots/day × 4 slots × funding collected per snipe — this makes the snapshot snipe a genuine recurring income
  stream rather than an occasional bonus.

  Phase 3 — Alpha Expansion (1-2 Months)

  3A. Funding Rate Decay Prediction

  Simple and VPS-friendly. Instead of entering whenever current_rate > 15%, predict what the rate will be at the next
  snapshot using the last 12 data points (now available at 1s cadence from WS).

  linear_rate = rate[-1] + (rate[-1] - rate[-12]) × (minutes_to_snapshot / 12min)
  if linear_rate > ENTRY_THRESHOLD: enter

  This prevents the most common losing scenario: entering at 20% when the rate is declining and actually settles at 8%,
  making the trade unprofitable after fees.

  No ML, no heavy computation — pure arithmetic on 12 floats. Lives comfortably in Python on a 1GB VPS.

  3B. Basis Trade Capture

  The perp premium (basis) is a second independent alpha source the bot currently treats as noise. When you enter a
  funding arb position:
  - You're long spot at price P_s
  - You're short perp at price P_p (where P_p > P_s when funding is positive)
  - The basis = (P_p - P_s) / P_s will converge toward 0 over time

  If you entered at basis = 0.05% and it converges to 0%, you make an additional 0.05% on notional from basis
  convergence, on top of funding payments.

  Plan: track entry_basis per position. Add a secondary exit condition: if basis has converged more than 50% of entry
  basis AND funding is still decent, consider holding. Conversely, if basis has widened, treat the extra convergence
  gain as a bonus exit trigger with a higher exit threshold.

  The basis_pnl_usd column already exists in trade_history but is never actively optimized. Start measuring it, then
  optimize entry basis selection.

  3C. Bybit Funding Rate Monitoring (Read-Only First)

  Add Bybit's public funding rate API (https://api.bybit.com/v5/market/tickers) as a read-only data source. No trading
  on Bybit initially — just use it to:

  1. Validate signals: if both Binance AND Bybit show elevated funding on SOLUSDT, the signal is much higher conviction
  than Binance alone
  2. Detect divergence: if Binance shows 30% but Bybit shows 5%, the funding may revert to Bybit's level soon — avoid
  entry or use a tighter basis stop
  3. Future cross-exchange arb: once the monitoring is live, full cross-exchange execution (Binance spot + Bybit perp or
   vice versa) becomes a natural extension

  One REST call every 60 seconds. No additional infrastructure needed.

  Phase 4 — Structural Advantages (2-3 Months)

  4A. VIP Fee Tier Progression

  VIP1 on Binance requires $1M/month spot volume at 0.04% spot maker (vs 0.056% now). At 4 positions rotating
  twice/month: 8 entries × $5k = $40k. That's far short.

  To legitimately reach VIP1, you need either more positions, more capital, or more frequent rotation. With Phase 2
  compounding and Phase 1 symbol expansion, this becomes achievable in 6-12 months as account grows.

  The fee reduction from VIP0+BNB (0.056%) to VIP1 (0.04%) is another ~29% reduction in spot fees. At $50k/month volume
  that saves ~$8/month — small now, meaningful at scale.

  4B. Kelly Criterion Position Sizing

  Instead of equal $2,500 per slot, size each position proportional to its Sharpe ratio based on:
  - Current annualized funding rate
  - Rate volatility over last 24 hours
  - Current basis deviation from mean
  - Liquidity depth (slippage risk)

  High-conviction setups (high rate + stable basis + deep liquidity) get 1.5-2× normal slot size. Weak setups get 0.5×.
  This concentrates capital where expected value is highest without changing overall risk profile.

  4C. Cross-Exchange Delta Neutral

  The ultimate fee reduction: instead of long spot + short perp (same exchange), do:
  - long perp on Exchange A + short perp on Exchange B

  When Bybit funding is 5% and Binance funding is 30%, you collect 25% annualized net by being long Bybit perp + short
  Binance perp. No spot leg at all = no 0.056% spot fees, no spot slippage, no margin borrowing cost.

  Requires Bybit API integration in the Rust execution engine. Large but high-value build.
